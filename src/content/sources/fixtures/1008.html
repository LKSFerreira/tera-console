Update

Dungeon

Rampaging RK-9 KennelLast Stand



Last Stand begins. Please check the separate announcement for details.
Entry is available via the existing RK-9 Kennel Dimensional Gem.
Dungeon matching and party recruitment are both available.
Records are only registered if the Boss 3 shield-break pattern (HARD transition) is completed.
Vanguard Request rewards can only be obtained if the Boss 3 shield-break pattern (HARD transition) is completed.
Item level is 950. Additional rewards including Darkshard can be obtained.
New patterns added per boss. HARD I system is not applied.
Known Issue — The enrage timing indicator based on boss HP does not match the actual timing.
Developer Note

This Last Stand features the Rampaging RK-9 Kennel. Patterns have been strengthened and reworked to emphasize party cooperation. The overall flow is similar to before, but records can vary significantly depending on how each boss's mechanics are handled.

In this Last Stand, if a party already ranked in the top positions makes a record after replacing only 1 or 2 members, that record will be excluded from title reward eligibility. This is to allow more players to receive rewards — as this issue was frequently observed in top 1–5 parties per region.

Accordingly, only the leaderboard will be published for now. Reward details will be announced later.

Additionally, Darkshard reward quantities have been increased, and the previously criticized entry count sharing restriction has been removed.

Other Changes
HARD I resurrection penalty reduced from 10× → 3×. (Applies to Last Stand as well)
HARD I ATK increase rate and Crit Power slightly decreased.
Developer Note

To improve HARD I rewards, regular materials have been added in place of Dungeon Core (Annihilation). While the rewards were already higher than those from Dungeon Core (Annihilation), we've heard that they still didn't feel adequate for the difficulty, so we've made adjustments.

We've also removed the Last Stand entry count sharing restriction, which should increase overall supply. We'll monitor the situation and make quick follow-up adjustments such as additional events as needed.

Additionally, as infinite resurrection by Priests and Mystics is no longer possible, we've reduced the resurrection penalty and recalibrated overall dungeon damage to ensure it doesn't feel excessive.

RK-9 Kennel: Ultimate leaderboard conditions changed — records only register after Boss 3's shield-break pattern is completed.

All skills and brooches reset upon dungeon reset or boss defeat in all dungeons.

Retribution — Active
Damage dealt on successful Retribution reduced by 90%.
Developer Note

The Retribution nerf is related to the Binding Scythes buff and the change that prevents Shadow Step from being removed by DoT damage, both added in the previous update.

Rather than buffing the assassination concept, having a DPS class rush forward to counter-attack and return was deemed contrary to our intended direction.

As Binding Scythes was found to provide satisfactory sustained damage after its update, and can partially replace the role of Retribution, we've made this adjustment.

Sorcerer relies on elemental chaining for its primary skills, and has a high proportion of skills without glyph effects. Combined with the recent Crit Resistance increase update, Crit hits were occurring less often than expected, so we've made adjustments.

We've also added Crit Factor increase to the Element Zero effect from Fusion skill hits, encouraging more Fusion usage and enabling more stable sustained DPS.

Priest
Grace of Resurrection — Focus Glyph cooldown reduction rate changed from % to a fixed value.
Divine Charge — Crit Power at max charge: 40% → 20%
Divine Charge — Power increase: 15 → 25
Divine Charge — Duration: 10s → 8s
Developer Note

Healers had difficulty gauging their own buff efficiency, making it hard to know how effective their investment was in practice. The detailed stats page has been updated to display this information, with plans to extend this to other classes in the future.

Until now, the healer meta allowed continuous resurrection even after mistakes, which reduced tension and the ability to differentiate player skill. This encouraged mindless repetitive play over actual tactical analysis, so adjustments were unavoidable.

Priest's Divine Charge has been adjusted to align with the class concept by applying Power increases, and to allow efficiency to scale with stat investment. Duration has also been adjusted to balance it against Mystic.

Rewards

Developer Note

While the low Darkshard drop rate was intentional, we observed that the time required to obtain them was making gameplay feel too loose. We'll increase Darkshard supply while reducing the drop rate of crafting recipes and production-related items.

We've also removed Disenchantment Neutralizer and Moonshine Fruit Potion (Non-Tradable) [Untradeable] from Pet Adventure, as they were overloading the loot pool.

Additionally, to prevent a single player from monopolizing rewards with an underfilled party, drop rewards will be significantly reduced when the party is short on members.

Vanguard Initiative Point Shop
Enchantment Powder price decreased.
Normal ~ Legendary Card Box prices slightly increased.
Blazing Essence of God / Scorching Essence of God prices increased.
Rune-related material prices adjusted.
Artifact Gemstone price decreased.
Consumable prices (excluding Ellinu's Tear) decreased.
Howling Rage gear item prices decreased.
Developer Note

Currently, most purchases from the Vanguard Initiative Point Shop are Card Boxes. We see this as a positive — lowering the barrier to card content. However, the core intent of the point system was to allow players to purchase what they actually need, rather than receiving unwanted items — to enable targeted exchanges and reduce accumulation.

As demand was concentrated on specific items, we considered removing them. However, since we didn't want to erase an already established culture, we instead raised the prices of high-demand items while increasing the amount of reputation points players can earn.

We hope this change leads to a smoother overall experience.
