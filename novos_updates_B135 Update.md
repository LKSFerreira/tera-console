B135 Update Notes
Hello, this is the TERA Console team.
Please find the details of the B135 update below.

Content
Dungeons
Defeating field monsters in level 500~700 dungeons now grants Vanguard Initiative reputation points.
The dungeon newbie bonus threshold has been increased from 5 clears to 15 clears.
If even one party member has fewer than 15 clears, the party receives an additional 50% reward.
Beginners gain a 25% increase to HP and Attack Power.
Applies to party dungeons with item level 500~850.
Vanguard Initiative reputation points have been added as drop rewards in the following dungeons.
Balder's Temple (3-Person)
Argon Corpus (Advanced)
Sky Cruiser Endeavor
Bathysmal Rise

Difficulty System
A difficulty system has been applied.
HARD I — Level 950 — Consumes 1 Condensed Dungeon Core
You can obtain Condensed Legacy of Annihilation and Condensed Dungeon Core.
You can obtain a large amount of Tier 1 Annihilation Material.
You can obtain Rune Material.
Rewards equivalent to a level 950 dungeon are granted.
There is no resurrection or recovery penalty.
HARD II — Level 1000 — Consumes 3 Condensed Dungeon Cores
You can obtain Condensed Legacy of Annihilation and Condensed Dungeon Core.
Drop rewards are increased by an additional +50%.
There is no resurrection or recovery penalty.
Darkshard Enhancement Stone is guaranteed to drop.

Matchmaking
Warriors can now queue for party matching in the Tanker role.

In Auto Matchmaking Dungeons, your own gear is now used if its item level is 900 or higher.

Glyphs
The calculation method for the "% reduced skill cooldown" effect has been changed to multiplicative.
Example — Using a 100-second skill with a Glyph effect that reduces cooldown by 30% (with a 70% total cooldown reduction already applied from gear)

Case	Remaining Time
Before (70% total gear reduction)	0 sec
After (70% total gear reduction)	21 sec
After gear-based cooldown reduction is applied, the Glyph's -30% is then calculated on the remaining 30 seconds.
Strength/Endurance Stat Display Changes
![Imagem exibindo os status](https://cs-live-static-psap.krapaas.com/console/tera/brand-site/admin/B135_stat.png)
The stat display format has been changed.
Before: Base + Total Increased Stat Value
After: Base + Gear Total Value + Buff Stat Value
Since buff stats are not included in scaling, this change is made to prevent confusion.
The white-colored stat value is the figure that actually generates scaling-based efficiency.

Healer Class Strength Scaling
The scaling starting threshold has been changed from 300 to 200.
A cap has been applied depending on the scaling range.
The cap does not apply to damage amplification stats.
For every 1 point of Strength, the following efficiency increase is applied.
Strength 200~500 range: 1%
Strength 500~600 range: 0.5%
Strength (Power)	Buff Amount Increase

300	+100%
400	+200%
500 (soft cap)	+300%
550	+325%
600 (hard cap)	+350%
650 or above (hard cap, no further increase)	+350%
Taunt
The duration of Taunt skills has been reduced by 50% compared to before. (36 sec → 18 sec)
After a Taunt skill is applied to a monster, it will not be taunted again by a Taunt skill for a set period of time.
Taunt skills from other classes are likewise subject to this immunity for the duration.
Natural aggro generated by a monster's HP decreasing remains unchanged at 36 seconds.
Tanker Class Endurance Scaling
Tanker classes now gain additional effects proportional to their Endurance stat.
Increased Taunt duration when using a Taunt skill
Increased uptime for Perfect Defense effects
Amplified damage reduction effect on weapon gear while blocking
Additional effects depending on class
The Tanker class Endurance stat threshold starts applying from 200.
No additional efficiency is granted beyond 550 Endurance.
